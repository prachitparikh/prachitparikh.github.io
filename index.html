What I do

The part between "we need this" and "it works"

Most of my work sits where requirements, data and testing meet. Here's what that looks like day to day.

Requirements

User stories with acceptance criteria a tester can't misread, and a clear line from each one to the test that proves it.

SQL data validation

Row counts, totals, duplicates and orphans checked before anyone signs off, so the numbers in the report are the numbers in the system.

UAT planning

Entry and exit criteria, test cases the business can run, and a defect process that keeps go-live on track.

AI at work

Writing specs an AI agent can build from, and checking what it produced against criteria set before the first run.

About Prachit Parikh

I'm a business analyst. Most of my work sits where requirements, data and testing meet: I take what stakeholders say they need and turn it into requirements and user stories a team can build and test, each with acceptance criteria and a clear line to the test that proves it.

I check the data behind those requirements in SQL and Excel, build reports in Power BI, and plan UAT so a change goes live the way the business expected. I was a business analyst at Banking Circle, working on data validation, traceability and UAT planning. Before that I worked in customer care at Elevance Health and did business systems analysis on an employee portal and an inventory system at Saklax Manufacturing.

These days I write about where business analysis is heading: AI agents that build from specs, data you can actually trust, and what the future of work looks like for people who translate between the business and the machines.
